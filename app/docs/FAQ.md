# Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

## General Questions

### What is MemeDAO?
MemeDAO is a decentralized autonomous organization (DAO) built on the Solana blockchain that combines meme culture with blockchain governance. Users can participate in community decisions through voting, own MEME tokens, and engage with the platform's features.

### How does MemeDAO work?
MemeDAO operates through a combination of smart contracts on the Solana blockchain and our web interface. Users connect their Solana wallets to participate in voting, view poll results, and manage their tokens.

### Is MemeDAO secure?
We prioritize security in all aspects of MemeDAO. Our smart contracts undergo thorough testing and review. However, all blockchain applications carry inherent risks, and users should exercise caution, use secure wallets, and never share private keys.

## Wallet and Connection

### Which wallets are supported?
MemeDAO currently supports Phantom and Solflare wallets. We plan to add support for additional Solana-compatible wallets in the future.

### How do I connect my wallet?
Click the "Connect Wallet" button in the top right corner of the application. Select your preferred wallet from the dropdown menu and follow the prompts to authorize the connection.

### What if I'm having trouble connecting my wallet?
If you're experiencing connection issues:
1. Ensure your wallet extension is installed and updated
2. Refresh the page and try connecting again
3. Check that you're on the correct network (Mainnet or Devnet)
4. Clear your browser cache and cookies
5. Try using a different supported browser

## Tokens

### What is the MEME token?
The MEME token is the native utility token of MemeDAO, used for governance, voting, and accessing platform features. It exists on the Solana blockchain as an SPL token.

### How can I get MEME tokens?
You can acquire MEME tokens by:
1. Purchasing them through our "Buy Token" page
2. Trading on supported decentralized exchanges like Raydium or Jupiter
3. Participating in community events or promotions

### What can I do with MEME tokens?
MEME tokens provide several utilities:
1. Voting rights on platform polls and proposals
2. Access to exclusive platform features
3. Participation in community governance
4. Potential for future use cases as determined by community governance

### Is there a maximum supply of MEME tokens?
Yes, MEME tokens have a fixed maximum supply of 1,000,000,000 tokens. This cap ensures scarcity and prevents inflation.

## Voting and Polls

### How does voting work?
Voting is done through our web interface. Users with connected wallets can navigate to active polls, select their preferred candidate, and submit their vote. Votes are recorded on the Solana blockchain and cannot be altered once submitted.

### Do I need tokens to vote?
Yes, you need to hold MEME tokens in your connected wallet to participate in voting. The weight of your vote may be proportional to your token holdings, depending on the specific poll configuration.

### Can I change my vote after submitting?
No, once a vote is submitted and confirmed on the blockchain, it cannot be changed or withdrawn. Please make your selection carefully.

### How are poll results calculated?
Poll results are calculated directly from the blockchain data, counting the number of votes for each candidate. Results are displayed in real-time on the Results page as votes are cast and confirmed.

### How long do polls remain active?
Poll duration varies and is set by the administrators for each individual poll. The remaining time is displayed on the active poll page.

## Technical Questions

### Which blockchain does MemeDAO use?
MemeDAO is built on the Solana blockchain, chosen for its high speed, low transaction costs, and scalability.

### What are the transaction fees?
MemeDAO does not charge fees for using the platform. However, users will need to pay standard Solana network fees (known as "gas fees") when making transactions. These fees are typically very low (fractions of a cent).

### Is MemeDAO open source?
Yes, our smart contracts and front-end application code are open source. You can find our repositories on our GitHub page.

## Account and Privacy

### How is my data stored and used?
MemeDAO collects minimal user data. Your wallet address and voting history are publicly visible on the blockchain. Please refer to our Privacy Policy for complete details on data collection and usage.

### Can I delete my account?
MemeDAO doesn't have traditional "accounts" - you interact with the platform through your wallet. You can disconnect your wallet at any time to stop using the platform, but transactions recorded on the blockchain cannot be deleted.

### Is my voting history private?
No, all votes are recorded on the public Solana blockchain and are visible to anyone. However, your vote is associated with your wallet address, not your personal identity.
