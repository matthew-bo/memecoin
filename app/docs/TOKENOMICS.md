## Vesting Schedule
- **Team Tokens**: 4-month cliff, then linear vesting over 24 months
- **Advisor Tokens**: 3-month cliff, then linear vesting over 18 months
- **Treasury**: 10% unlocked at launch, remaining vested over 36 months
- **Community & Voting**: Released gradually based on platform milestones and voting participation
- **Liquidity & Airdrops**: Available at launch

## Token Utility
1. **Governance**: Token holders can vote on platform decisions and proposals
2. **Voting Power**: More tokens = more voting weight in platform polls
3. **Rewards**: Earn tokens by participating in votes and creating winning poll proposals
4. **Platform Fees**: Reduced fees for token holders based on holdings
5. **Premium Features**: Access to exclusive content and voting opportunities

## Deflationary Mechanisms
- **Vote Burning**: Small percentage of tokens used for voting (0.1%) are burned
- **Buy & Burn**: 1% of platform revenue used to buy and burn tokens
- **Staking Rewards**: Incentivize long-term holding through staking rewards
